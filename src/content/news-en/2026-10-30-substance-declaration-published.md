The subschema "Substance Declaration" has been approved as version v2.0.0 and is now available in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/VDA231-301/VDA_231-301__SubstanceDeclaration).

It extends VDA 231-301 with a building block for describing substance-related information in a structured, machine-readable way and exchanging it along the supply chain. This allows substance declarations to be transmitted together with material and test data within the same data model.

The subschema was published for discussion in April 2026. Feedback from the community has been incorporated into the version now approved by the VDA project group.

Feedback and comments remain welcome as an [issue](https://github.com/VDA231-301/VDA_231-301__SubstanceDeclaration/issues).
